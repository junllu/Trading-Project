# Pre-registration — short-term reversal (v3)

Committed **before** the holdout names are examined for anything. The git
timestamp is the proof. Changing this after results exist makes a new study.

## Where the hypothesis came from (honestly)

It was NOT pre-registered in v1 or v2. It emerged from their results:
- v1 (2015–2022, 90 names): breakdowns in weak names in down markets were
  followed by outperformance (bd55+relstr+market@10, t −2.24 as scored).
- v2 (2023–2026, 312 names): the t distribution skewed negative — 37 combos
  at t ≤ −2 vs ~12 expected by chance; 20-day breakouts faded over 5 days
  (bo20+trend+squeeze@5, t −3.32).
Because the idea was formed by looking at those 312 names, they cannot test
it. The only clean data left is the **221 holdout names** in
`2026-09-27-breakout-regime-universe.json`, never examined in any study.

## Hypotheses (6 trials, fixed)

Triggers and conditions exactly as defined in v1/v2 (`breakout_study.py`,
`breakout_regime_study.py`). Direction is REVERSED from v1/v2:

| id | event | position | horizon |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | bd20 | long (bounce) | 5 |
| R2 | bd20 | long (bounce) | 10 |
| R3 | bd55 | long (bounce) | 5 |
| R4 | bd55 + relstr(weak) + market(down) | long (bounce) | 10 |
| F1 | bo20 | avoid/trim (underperforms) | 5 |
| F2 | bo20 + trend + squeeze | avoid/trim (underperforms) | 5 |

Scored as excess over the same-day holdout-universe average, next-open entry,
net of retail round-trip cost (also shown at 3×), t on non-overlapping
h-session blocks. Long-only use: R = buy the dip; F = do not chase / trim.

## Pass rule (holdout names only)

1. Full period (2015/2018 → latest): blocks ≥ 30, mean net > 0,
   **t ≥ 2.4** (one-sided 5% with a Bonferroni correction for 6 trials).
2. Consistency: mean net > 0 in BOTH eras separately (≤ 2022-12-31 with a
   20-session purge, and ≥ 2023-01-01), each with t ≥ 1.0.

Passing both → the combo goes to the forward paper test (P3). Failing is a
valid result. All 6 trials are recorded to `data/trials.jsonl`.
