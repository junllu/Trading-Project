# Pre-registration — mega-cap momentum v2, point-in-time S&P 500 membership

Committed before the run. Implemented by `app/backtest/momentum_v2.py`.

## Why v2

v1 (commit e6a9350) showed 41–46% CAGR, but diagnostics traced it to
selection bias: the current S&P list includes names added AFTER they rallied
(TSLA 2020-12, SMCI 2024-03, PLTR 2024-09), which momentum buys years early.

## Universe

At each rebalance date, only tickers that were S&P 500 members ON THAT DATE,
per `data/universe/sp500_ticker_start_end.csv` (github.com/fja05680/sp500,
fetched 2026-09-27). Renamed tickers take their old ticker's membership:
META←FB, ELV←ANTM, COR←ABC, BALL←BLL, DAY←CDAY, FI←FISV, RTX←UTX,
WBD←DISCA, PARA←VIAC/CBS, BKNG←PCLN. Former members with a price feed are
included (119 of 250 removed since 2016); those without a feed (mostly
acquisitions) are not — the remaining bias is stated, not hidden.

## Rules (unchanged from v1)

Every 21 sessions: rank point-in-time members by trailing 63-session average
dollar volume; from the top 50 (M2-50) or top 100 (M2-100), hold the 10 with
the best return from t−252 to t−21, equal weight; turnover pays retail
one-way cost. Window 2016-01-04 → latest; eras split 2023-01-01. **2 trials.**

## Pass rule

Under the user's bar (2026-09-27: "average positive"): CAGR > 0 in both eras
AND Sharpe > SPY's in both eras (a strategy that earns less per unit of risk
than the index is not worth running instead of it). Passing ⇒ paper track.
