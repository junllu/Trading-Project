# Pre-registration — wheel1: cash-secured puts → shares → covered calls (paper)

Committed before the first paper trade. Forward test only, on REAL Robinhood
option quotes (read-only). No historical option prices exist here, so there
is no backtest; the forward record is the evidence. Engine:
`app/paper/wheel.py`. Any rule change is a new version (wheel2), run alongside.

## Capital and sizing
- $50,000 paper. One contract (100 shares) per name; one position per name.
- New puts only while cash − put collateral − the new collateral ≥ 10% of equity.

## Universe and exclusions (checked before quoting)
- rev1's 533 names with last close between $20 and $250.
- Excluded: a 20- or 55-session breakdown in the last 5 sessions (S3: tails
  are fatter right after breakdowns); earnings on or before the expiry
  (Robinhood earnings calendar).
- Each session, 8 eligible names are drawn at random (seeded by the date) to
  be quoted — never "the juiciest premiums", which would be selection on noise.

## Selling puts
- Expiry 30–45 days out (first listed expiry ≥ 30 days; skip if none ≤ 45).
- Delta between −0.20 and −0.30, closest to −0.25.
- IV ≥ **1.2 ×** trailing 20-session realised vol (S3 break-even ≈ 0.99, plus margin).
- Bid ≥ $0.30 and (ask − bid) / mid ≤ 25%.
- Fill at the BID. Fee $0.05 per contract per transaction.

## Managing
- Buy back at the ASK when ask ≤ 50% of the credit received, or when 21 or
  fewer days remain.
- Expiry: settled on the underlying's official close on the expiry date —
  below the put strike ⇒ assigned 100 shares at the strike; above a call
  strike ⇒ called away at the strike. Early assignment is not modelled.

## Covered calls on assigned shares
- Same expiry window; delta +0.20 to +0.30 closest to 0.25; strike ≥ cost
  basis (put strike − put credit); IV ≥ **1.3 ×** realised vol (S3 call
  break-even ≈ 1.11, plus margin); same liquidity rules. If none qualifies,
  hold the shares and look again next session.
- Risk stop: shares ≥ 25% below cost basis are sold at the live price, and
  any open call on them is bought back.

## Measurement
Daily equity = cash + shares at live price − short options at mark.
Benchmarks: SPY bought with $50,000 on day one, and the same names held from
each put's sale date. Scorecard: equity curve, premium collected, assignment
rate vs model delta, closed-cycle return, max drawdown.

## Pass rule (for live consideration, after ≥ 40 sessions and ≥ 20 closed cycles)
Beats SPY buy-and-hold on return AND on max drawdown over the same window,
with no single cycle losing more than 10% of paper capital.
